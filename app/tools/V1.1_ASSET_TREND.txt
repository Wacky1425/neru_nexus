Neru Nexus V1.1-1 Asset Snapshot / Trend

- R_AssetSnapshots is auto-created on first use.
- Daily 23:00 Apps Script trigger is installed by runReleaseChecks().
- Snapshot stores assets, liabilities, net assets, cash, investment and other assets.
- Asset page > 資産推移 shows 6 months / 1 year / all period.
- Current snapshot can be captured manually from the app.
- Daily capture attempts investment price refresh first.
- No manual spreadsheet migration is required.

After GAS deployment:
1. Run runReleaseChecks() once.
2. Confirm regression and integrity checks pass.
3. Open Asset > 資産推移 and tap the top-right snapshot button once to create the first point.
