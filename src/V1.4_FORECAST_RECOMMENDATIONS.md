# V1.4 Forecasting / Recommendations

Implemented:
- Month-end variable-spend projection from elapsed-day pace.
- Month-end total expense and balance estimate.
- Safe daily spending guideline for the remaining variable budget.
- Credit-card payment forecast surfaced on Home.
- Alerts for overspending pace, thin card-payment cash, and low emergency cash.
- Approved recurring payments are flagged when the current amount is >=15% and >=300 yen above historical average.
- NISA recommendation uses the existing allocation result and is suppressed by unsafe spending/cash conditions.
- Home shows a single forecast/recommendation card.
- No spreadsheet migration required.

Notes:
- Forecasts are estimates for planning.
- The NISA amount is a recommendation based on configured Neru Nexus rules, not investment advice.
