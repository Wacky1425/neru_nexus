# V1.3-3 Business / Tax Preparation Follow-up

Implemented:
- Annual operational readiness summary for business transactions.
- Detects:
  - status == 要確認
  - generic / missing category classification
  - missing evidence URL for business expenses
  - business expense with expense ratio 0%
- Unique issue count is deduplicated per transaction.
- Monthly readiness counts and score are returned with monthly summaries.
- Business report UI now shows:
  - readiness score
  - unique transactions needing attention
  - breakdown by review / classification / evidence / expense ratio
  - monthly remaining-issue counts
- Existing tax CSV export format is unchanged.
- No spreadsheet migration is required.

Important:
- The readiness score is an application workflow score only.
- It does not determine whether a tax return is legally complete or correct.
